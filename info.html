How the days are structured

Each day pairs a short talk with a longer hands-on block — you'll spend more time writing circuits than listening to slides. Mentors from the Qiskit community are on the floor throughout the labs to help you debug, not just demo.

No coding background in quantum computing is assumed. If you know basic Python and a little linear algebra, you're ready.
