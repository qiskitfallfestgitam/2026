Important Information

The essentials before you plan your visit — full details, FAQs and policies live on the info page.

  • Eligibility Open to all undergraduate & postgraduate students — no prior quantum computing experience required.
  • Team size Individual or teams of up to 4, depending on the track.
  • What to bring Laptop, college ID, and a curiosity for linear algebra.
  • Fee Free for GITAM students; nominal fee for external participants.
